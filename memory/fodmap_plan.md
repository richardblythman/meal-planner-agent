# FODMAP Sensitivity Testing Plan

A structured approach to identifying FODMAP triggers, specifically designed for IBS-D (diarrhea-predominant).

---

## Overview

### What are FODMAPs?

**F**ermentable **O**ligosaccharides, **D**isaccharides, **M**onosaccharides **A**nd **P**olyols - short-chain carbohydrates that are poorly absorbed and rapidly fermented in the gut.

### The 5 FODMAP Categories

| Category | Type | Common Sources | Why Problematic |
|----------|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Fructans** | Oligosaccharide | Garlic, onions, wheat, leeks | No human enzymes to digest |
| **GOS** | Oligosaccharide | Legumes, beans, lentils | No human enzymes to digest |
| **Lactose** | Disaccharide | Milk, soft cheese, yogurt | Many lack sufficient lactase |
| **Fructose** | Monosaccharide | Apples, pears, honey, mango | Only when exceeds glucose |
| **Polyols** | Sugar alcohols | Mushrooms, cauliflower, stone fruits, sugar-free products | Osmotic/laxative effect |

### IBS-D Specific Considerations

- FODMAPs draw water into intestines (osmotic effect) - worsens diarrhea
- **Polyols and fructose** produce the strongest osmotic effect
- IBS-D patients have the **highest response rate** (86%) to low-FODMAP elimination
- Most common triggers for IBS-D: Fructans (56%), Mannitol (54%), GOS (35%)

### Personal Notes

- **Lactose**: Tolerated - dairy is not a trigger
- **Fructose (honey)**: Tolerated - can keep using honey

## Phase 1: Elimination (2-6 weeks)

**Goal**: Remove all high-FODMAP foods to establish symptom baseline.

### Foods to AVOID

#### Vegetables (High-FODMAP)
| Food | FODMAP Type | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Garlic | Fructans | Use garlic-infused oil instead |
| Onions (ALL types) | Fructans | Green part of spring onions OK |
| Cauliflower | Mannitol | High at any serving |
| Mushrooms | Mannitol | Very high polyols |
| Artichokes | Fructans | - |
| Asparagus (>42g) | Fructans | Small amounts may be OK |
| Leeks (white part) | Fructans | Green part safer |
| Sugar snap peas (>5 pods) | GOS/Fructans | Limit to 16g |

#### Fruits
- Apples, pears (fructose/sorbitol)
- Mangoes, cherries, watermelon (fructose)
- Stone fruits: peaches, plums, nectarines, apricots (sorbitol)
- Dried fruits

#### Grains
- Wheat, rye, barley (in large amounts)

#### Legumes
- Beans, lentils, chickpeas (GOS)

#### Other
- Honey, agave, high-fructose corn syrup
- Sugar-free products (contain polyols)

### Foods that are SAFE

#### Vegetables (Low-FODMAP Servings)

| Vegetable | Safe Serving | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Carrots (orange, mature) | 500g (~5 medium) | Low FODMAP |
| Baby carrots | 120g (~5 small) | >120g contains sorbitol, mannitol, fructans, GOS |
| Spinach | 75g | ✓ Currently in your rotation |
| Broccoli florets | 75g (3/4 cup) | ✓ Currently in your rotation |
| Zucchini/Courgette | 50-65g | Above 85g = high fructans |
| Eggplant | 75g | Safe choice |
| Green bell pepper | 75g | Very safe |
| Cucumber | Unlimited | ✓ Currently in your rotation |
| Tomatoes (common) | 75g | Low FODMAP, but nightshade |
| Green beans | 75g (~15 beans) | Safe |
| Potatoes | Unlimited | Low FODMAP (but nightshade) |
| Sweet potato | 75g | ✓ Currently in your rotation |
| Bok choy | 75g | Safe alternative |
| Lettuce | Unlimited | Any type |

#### Proteins
All plain proteins are naturally FODMAP-free:
- Chicken, beef, fish, seafood ✓
- Eggs ✓
- Tofu (firm, drained)

#### Grains/Starches
- Rice (all types) ✓
- Quinoa ✓
- Oats (certified GF if also gluten-sensitive)
- Potatoes ✓

#### Fruits (Low-FODMAP)
- Bananas (unripe/slightly green) ✓
- Kiwi ✓
- Oranges, grapes, strawberries
- Blueberries (40g limit)
- Raspberries (60g / ⅓ cup limit)

#### Dairy
- All dairy is fine (lactose tolerated)

**Safe Recipes/Components:**
- Salmon (plain protein) ✓
- Chicken breast ✓
- Rice, quinoa ✓
- Broccoli (keep to 75g) ✓

### Flavor Substitutions for Elimination Phase

| Instead of... | Use... |
|---------------|--------|
| Garlic | Garlic-infused olive oil (FODMAPs don't dissolve in oil) |
| Onion | Green part of spring onions, chives, asafoetida |
| Onion (bulk) | Fennel bulb, celery |
| Honey | Maple syrup (pure) |

### End of Phase 1: Evaluating Success

**When to evaluate**: After 2-6 weeks of strict elimination.

**Signs it's working (proceed to Phase 2):**
- [ ] Reduced diarrhea frequency
- [ ] Improved stool consistency (Bristol scale moving toward 3-4)
- [ ] Less urgency
- [ ] Reduced bloating/gas
- [ ] Less abdominal pain

**How much improvement?** Aim for ~50-75% symptom reduction. You don't need to be 100% symptom-free.

**Signs it's NOT working (after 6 weeks):**
- No meaningful change in symptoms
- FODMAPs may not be your main trigger
- Consider: stress, other food intolerances, SIBO, etc.

**Before moving to Phase 2, confirm:**
- [ ] Strict elimination for at least 2 weeks
- [ ] Symptoms have noticeably improved
- [ ] You have a clear baseline to compare against during reintroduction

## Phase 2: Reintroduction (8-12 weeks)

**Goal**: Systematically test each FODMAP category to identify YOUR specific triggers.

### Testing Protocol

**For each FODMAP group:**
1. Day 1: Test 1/4 serving
2. Day 2: Test 1/2 serving
3. Day 3: Test full serving
4. Days 4-6: Rest period (strict low-FODMAP)
5. Day 7+: Begin next challenge

**Important**: Continue eating low-FODMAP background diet throughout all testing.

### Recommended Testing Order for IBS-D

Test polyols first (most common IBS-D triggers), then fructans:

| Week | FODMAP | Test Food | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 |
|------|--------|-----------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Mannitol | Mushrooms | 14g | 50g | 75g |
| 2 | Sorbitol | Avocado | 20g | 40g | 80g |
| 3 | Fructans (veg) | Garlic | 1/4 clove | 1/2 clove | 1 clove |
| 4 | Fructans (veg) | Onion | 1 tbsp | 2 tbsp | 1/4 onion |
| 5 | Fructans (grain) | Wheat bread | 1/2 slice | 1 slice | 2 slices |
| 6 | Fructose | Mango | 9g | 20g | 30g |
| 7 | GOS | Canned beans | 45g | 65g | 100g |

*Lactose skipped - already known to be tolerated.*

### Symptom Tracking During Reintroduction

Use your existing symptom_log.md with these additions:

```markdown
### [DATE] - FODMAP Reintroduction

**Challenge**: [FODMAP type] - [Food] - [Amount]
**Day of challenge**: 1/2/3 or Rest day

**Bristol Scale**: X/7

**Symptoms within 24 hours**:
- Bloating (0-3):
- Gas (0-3):
- Abdominal pain (0-3):
- Urgency (0-3):
- Diarrhea (Y/N):

**Verdict**: Pass / Moderate / Fail
```

### Interpreting Results

| Result | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Pass** | No symptoms at full dose | Can include freely |
| **Moderate** | Symptoms at high dose only | Include in small amounts |
| **Fail** | Symptoms even at low dose | Avoid or use very sparingly |

## FODMAP Stacking Warning

Even low-FODMAP foods can trigger symptoms when combined in one meal.

**Example Problem Meal**:
- Broccoli (75g - at limit) +
- Zucchini (65g - at limit) +
- Sweet potato (75g - at limit)
= Combined fructans may exceed tolerance

**Solution**: Don't combine multiple vegetables at their upper limits in one meal.

## Hidden FODMAP Sources (IBS-D Alert)

Check these carefully - often contain polyols:

- [ ] Protein bars/powders (often contain inulin, chicory root)
- [ ] Sugar-free gum, mints, candy (sorbitol, mannitol, xylitol)
- [ ] Medications/supplements (sorbitol as fillers)
- [ ] Sauces, marinades (onion, garlic powder)
- [ ] Stock cubes/broth (often contain onion)
- [ ] Pre-made spice blends
